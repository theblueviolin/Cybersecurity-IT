📖 Phishing Awareness Training

Your Task: Review each email and determine if it is legitimate or a phishing attempt. Click the appropriate button to classify each email. You'll receive feedback and learn why emails are classified as phishing.

Common Phishing Indicators to Look For:

  • Urgency Language: "Immediate action required," "Your account will be locked," "Limited time"
  • Suspicious Domains: Domain names that misspell legitimate companies (paypa1 instead of paypal)
  • Requests for Sensitive Info: Asking for passwords, credit cards, or personal data via email
  • Impersonation: Claiming to be from banks, PayPal, Amazon, Microsoft, etc.
  • Suspicious Links: Hover to see actual URL before clicking
  • Poor Grammar/Formatting: Professional companies have QA processes
  • Generic Greetings: "Dear User" instead of your actual name (legitimate companies know you)
🎓 Why Phishing Defense Matters

Phishing is the most common attack vector for credential theft and ransomware deployment. In 2023, over 90% of data breaches involved phishing. Security awareness training is critical for:

  • Preventing unauthorized access to company systems
  • Protecting customer data and privacy
  • Avoiding ransomware and malware infections
  • Meeting compliance requirements (HIPAA, GDPR, SOC 2)
  • Reducing incident response costs
