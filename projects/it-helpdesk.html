What is IT Support?

IT Help Desk professionals provide first-line support for end-user issues. They triage problems, document issues, and either resolve them directly or escalate to specialized teams.

Ticket System Workflow:

  1. Reception: User submits ticket describing issue
  2. Categorization: Ticket type and priority assigned
  3. Triage: Help desk analyst reviews and creates action plan
  4. Resolution: Analyst follows troubleshooting steps to resolve issue
  5. Escalation: Complex issues escalated to specialized teams
  6. Documentation: Solutions documented in knowledge base
  7. Closure: Ticket closed when issue resolved

Priority Levels:

  • CRITICAL: System down, no workaround available. Immediate response required.
  • HIGH: Significant impact on productivity. Expedited response.
  • MEDIUM: Moderate impact. Address within 24 hours.
  • LOW: Minimal impact. Handle during normal business hours.

Common Issues & Skills:

Help desk professionals need knowledge of password resets, network connectivity (WiFi/Ethernet), printer troubleshooting, performance optimization, and software troubleshooting. Strong communication skills are essential for explaining technical solutions to non-technical users.
